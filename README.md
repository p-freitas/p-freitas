## Working with AI

I build tools around AI coding assistants and run local multi-agent workflows to ship, test, and share personal projects faster — from monitoring subscription quotas across Claude Code, Codex, and Google Antigravity to automating release previews and social updates.

## SaaS

### [NoCardápio](https://www.nocardapio.com.br)

A digital menu and online ordering SaaS for restaurants and food shops, built around a flat subscription model with **0% commission** on orders (merchants handle payments and delivery directly).

- **Real-time menu & stock:** Manage categories, products, photos, descriptions, pricing, and availability from one dashboard.
- **Visual customization:** Includes 6 preset themes and 7 customizable colors with live preview across the menu, cart, modals, and reviews.
- **Orders & growth tools:** 4-step checkout, real-time order tracking with instant notifications, sales analytics, customer reviews, and custom discount coupons.
